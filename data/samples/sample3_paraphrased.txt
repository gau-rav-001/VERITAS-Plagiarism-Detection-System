The field of artificial intelligence is completely reshaping the behavior of machines and their ability to choose and decide. Learning systems that rely on statistics process enormous volumes of information in order to uncover trends that no person could ever detect with the naked eye. Brain-inspired computing architectures can now recognize objects in pictures, comprehend spoken words, and convert text between languages. Sophisticated neural architectures stack numerous processing stages to derive features at ever higher levels of abstraction from unprocessed input. Consequently, intelligent programs have found their way into medicine, banking, transport, and schooling. Still, worries over confidentiality of personal data, unfair treatment by algorithms, and elimination of human jobs remain serious obstacles that scientists have to tackle in a careful and ethical manner.
