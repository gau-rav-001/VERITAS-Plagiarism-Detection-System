Artificial intelligence is changing the way machines learn and make decisions. Machine learning algorithms analyze large datasets to identify patterns that would be impossible for humans to find manually. Neural networks, inspired by the structure of the human brain, are capable of recognizing objects, understanding speech, and translating languages. Deep learning models use multiple layers of computation to extract increasingly abstract features from raw data. As a result, AI systems are now deployed across healthcare, finance, transportation, and education. However, concerns about data privacy, algorithmic bias, and job displacement remain significant challenges that researchers must address responsibly.
