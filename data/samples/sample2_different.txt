Climate change is one of the most pressing environmental issues of the twenty-first century. Rising global temperatures are causing polar ice caps to melt and sea levels to climb, threatening coastal communities around the world. Scientists agree that greenhouse gas emissions from burning fossil fuels are the primary driver of this warming trend. Renewable energy sources such as solar, wind, and hydropower offer a cleaner alternative to coal and oil. International agreements like the Paris Accord aim to limit temperature rise by encouraging nations to reduce their carbon footprint. Yet progress remains slow, and developing countries face the difficult task of balancing economic growth with environmental protection.
